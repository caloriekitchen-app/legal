Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026 (v1.6)

This Privacy Policy explains how CalorieKitchen ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use the CalorieKitchen mobile application ("App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Data Controller

The data controller for your personal data is the individual developer of CalorieKitchen. You can contact us with any data-related query at:

caloriekitchen@gmail.com

2. What Data We Collect

The data we collect depends on how you use the App.

If you sign in with Google:

  • Account data: Your Google email address and display name, as provided by Google Sign-In.
  • Profile data: Age, biological sex, height, weight, activity level, dietary goal, and preferred units — entered during onboarding or updated in Settings.
  • Diary data: Food entries (name, quantity, macros, calories), meal type, and date. Exercise entries and water intake logs.
  • Weight logs: Body-weight entries you manually log or that are imported from a connected health app.
  • Meal templates & fridge: Custom meal templates and fridge/shopping lists you create.
  • Custom foods: Any foods you add that are not in our database.

If you connect Health Connect (Android):

  • Steps, exercise sessions, active and total calories burned, and body-weight readings from Health Connect. When you grant permission, CalorieKitchen reads these values to populate your diary. If you use a signed-in account, imported diary entries sync with your account; Guest Mode keeps them on your device.

If you use Guest Mode:

  • Your diary and profile data stays on your device and is not synced to your CalorieKitchen account while you use Guest Mode. Ads and crash reporting may still send limited device or diagnostic information to their providers as described below.

If you use an AI premium feature (Bronze, Silver, or Gold subscription):

  • Meal/fridge photos: When you request meal or fridge scanning, a resized photo is sent through our backend to Google Gemini to identify food or ingredients. CalorieKitchen does not persist the photo in its backend database. Google handles API inputs under its own Gemini API terms, which can include safety and abuse-monitoring retention; this is not a promise that every provider immediately deletes the photo.
  • AI insight context: Recipe requests send fridge ingredient names, dietary preference, goal and recent food-name trends. For eligible subscribers, opening the Progress screen can automatically request an AI observation using aggregated calorie/protein, weight-trend, milestone and exercise/step statistics, including statistics derived from Health Connect imports. The full raw diary and weight history are not sent in that request. Local diary/fridge insight cards do not use a remote AI model.
  • Food-recognition feedback: Meal scanning records recognised food labels, matching results, estimated portions and feedback/corrections in our food-intelligence backend. Initial scan observations use a pseudonym; correction observations do not add a person identifier. This is separate from storing a photo.

Account, advertising and diagnostic information:

  • Subscriptions: You must sign in with Google before purchasing or restoring. RevenueCat receives the Supabase account UUID as the app user ID, plus store purchase and entitlement information. This is a pseudonymous account identifier, not an anonymous identity. Google Play handles the payment; CalorieKitchen does not receive your payment-card or bank-account details.
  • Device identifiers and diagnostics: An Android device identifier and first-seen timestamp are stored in our backend to maintain the guest advertising grace period across reinstallations. AdMob and Sentry may also process identifiers, IP addresses and technical diagnostics. These transmissions can occur without cloud diary sync.

3. Why We Collect Your Data (Lawful Basis)

  • Contractual necessity: To provide the core features of the App — calculating your calorie targets, syncing your diary across devices, and restoring your data on a new device.
  • Legitimate interests: To maintain app stability, debug issues, and improve features. We do not build advertising profiles or sell your data.
  • Consent: You choose whether to connect Health Connect and which read permissions to grant. Imported steps, exercise sessions, calories burned and weight can become diary/weight entries; signed-in cloud sync can upload those entries. Disconnecting stops future imports but does not remove existing imported copies. Revoke Android permissions in Health Connect and delete copied entries or request account deletion separately.
  • Consent (AI features): Payment alone is not consent to processing health information. Photo scanning and recipe generation run when requested; eligible progress observations can be requested automatically when the Progress screen opens. These features use the data described in section 2. No free-form chat is offered.

4. How We Store Your Data

The app keeps a local copy of your profile, diary and lists for offline use. Signing in with Google enables cloud account storage and sync through Supabase. Supabase also hosts a separate food-intelligence database. Server requests use HTTPS; this is transport encryption, not end-to-end encryption of your cloud account data.

Our providers may process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Provider handling depends on the service and its applicable terms. Contact caloriekitchen@gmail.com for information about the processors and transfer safeguards applicable to your data.

An in-app account deletion request starts a 7-day cooling-off period. You can cancel the request while it is pending. After that period, scheduled cleanup attempts to delete your account and associated cloud entries, AI cache and usage records, and unlink linked food-intelligence observations. Ownership verification or cleanup failures can delay completion; contact us if your request remains pending. Historical subscription identifiers require verified reconciliation before erasure.

Accounts without a sign-in or synced update for 12 consecutive months are eligible for scheduled deletion using the same cleanup safeguards. Guest diary data remains local until you clear it, clear app storage or uninstall. The separate server device first-seen record can outlast a guest reinstall; contact us about that record.

AI results are cached to avoid repeated generation. Cache entries older than 30 days are pruned when that account makes an insight request; this is not a guarantee of deletion at exactly 30 days. Food-recognition observations can remain after their person link is removed. Store billing records and provider security/diagnostic records may follow their providers’ retention rules or legal obligations.

5. Who We Share Your Data With

We do not sell your diary or profile data. The following services help operate CalorieKitchen or handle information under their own applicable policies.

Services used to operate the app:

  • Supabase: Hosts our database; stores your account, profile, diary, and other in-app data you create on our behalf.
  • RevenueCat: Manages subscription and purchase status using your Supabase account UUID, store purchase history and entitlements. Google sign-in in CalorieKitchen is required; the Google Play account used for billing is a separate store account.
  • Sentry: Receives crash/error reports, stack traces, device/OS details, performance measurements and automatic diagnostic breadcrumbs. We do not intentionally attach your diary, profile or photos to reports. Diagnostic context can include information contained in error messages and breadcrumbs.
  • AI service provider: Google Gemini processes photos and aggregated insight context described in section 2. Its API terms govern provider processing and safety retention; see https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms. CalorieKitchen does not persist uploaded photos in its backend database.

Third parties — also use data for their own stated purposes:

  • Google: For authentication via Google Sign-In. Google's use of your data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • USDA FoodData Central: Meal scanning can send a recognised food name to this public nutrition service to look up calorie/macro values. We do not send your account UUID, full diary or photo to USDA.
  • Google AdMob: Ads may involve IP addresses (including approximate-location inference), advertising/app identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Where available, review consent in Settings → Advertising and use Android advertising-ID controls. Non-personalised ads do not necessarily mean no data collection. Active ad-free plans prevent third-party ad display; this does not guarantee no consent-SDK communication.

Law enforcement or courts:

We may disclose data where compelled by a valid court order, subpoena, or other legal process, or where necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of us, our users, or the public.

  • Open Food Facts and GitHub Pages: Barcode lookup and external food images can contact Open Food Facts. Legal documents and catalogue updates can be retrieved from GitHub Pages. These providers receive ordinary web-request information such as IP address; a barcode lookup sends the product code, not your cloud diary.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

The App does not use browser cookies. It uses device-local storage (AsyncStorage and SQLite) to store your diary and profile data locally for offline access. This is necessary for the App to function.

7. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: Ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure: Ask us to delete your account and all associated data. You can also request this via Settings → Delete account & data in the App.
  • Right to portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Right to restriction: Ask us to restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

To exercise your rights, contact caloriekitchen@gmail.com. We normally respond within one month; we may need proportionate identity verification and will explain any permitted extension. Never send passwords, verification codes or payment-card details.

Request CalorieKitchen account and data deletion without reinstalling the app

8. California & Other US State Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you may have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to request its deletion or correction, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

We do not sell personal data, and the only data shared for advertising purposes is the limited AdMob disclosure in section 5, which you can opt out of via your device's ad settings or in-app ad preferences. To exercise any CCPA or other US state privacy rights, contact us at caloriekitchen@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at anyone under 18. We ask users to declare their age and confirm adult eligibility; this is a self-declaration, not verified proof of age. The eligibility confirmation is stored with the profile and, for signed-in users, in their account backup. We do not knowingly permit anyone under 18 to use the App or its AI features. If you believe someone under 18 has provided personal data, contact us or use our account-deletion page to request deletion. Existing saved data is not automatically erased by the eligibility check.
